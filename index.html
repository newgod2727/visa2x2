Questions people actually ask

Does my photo get uploaded anywhere?

No. The cropping, the resizing and the JPEG compression all run inside this page on your own device. There is no server that receives the picture, no gallery, no account and no backup. When you close the tab it is gone. The only thing that ever leaves your device is the payment, and that goes to the blockchain, not to us.

Why can I not pay with a card?

Card processing needs a merchant account this site does not have yet. Today it takes one stablecoin on one network, which keeps the price at 4 instead of 4 plus fees. If you have no wallet, this is not the right site for you and you should not waste your time on it.

Can you fix the background for me?

No, and that is deliberate. Since January 2026 the US State Department rejects photos where the background has been digitally replaced. A tool that paints a white wall behind you is selling you a rejection. Stand in front of a real plain wall instead.

Will it retouch my face?

Never. No smoothing, no whitening, no eye enlarging, no generated face. The photo you upload is the photo you get back, only cropped, resized and compressed.

Does this work for a Schengen or a passport photo?

Not yet. Right now this makes one thing: the US 2 x 2 at 600 x 600 pixels.

My DS-160 says the photo failed. What now?

Read what it says. If it names the dimensions, the aspect ratio or the file size, that is on this tool and you get your 4 USDC back. If it names your expression, your glasses, the lighting or the background, retake the photo and run it through again at no extra cost within 30 days, using the same transaction hash.
